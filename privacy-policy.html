Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

What FM2025 Is

FM2025 is a personal content-automation tool. It generates AI-assisted short-form car and music videos and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account. It is operated by a single individual for their own channel and is not offered as a public service or made available to other end users.

Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account to FM2025 through TikTok's own login, FM2025 receives:

  • An access token and refresh token scoped to the permissions you approve (video upload and publishing).
  • Basic creator account information needed to confirm which account a video is being posted to (such as your TikTok username and avatar).

FM2025 does not access your TikTok direct messages, contacts, browsing history, or any data beyond what the requested scopes provide.

How We Use It

The access token is used exclusively to upload and publish video files that the operator has already created to the connected TikTok account, at the operator's own initiation. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond posting the operator's own content.

Data Storage & Retention

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device, not on a shared or third-party server. Tokens are retained only for as long as the account remains connected and are deleted when the operator disconnects the account or revokes access from their TikTok settings.

Data Sharing

FM2025 does not sell, rent, or share any account data or tokens with third parties. No data collected through this integration is used for any purpose other than posting the operator's own videos to their own account.

Your Rights

You may revoke FM2025's access to your TikTok account at any time from TikTok's own account settings (Settings → Security → Manage account access). Revoking access immediately invalidates any stored token.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app operator via the contact details on file with TikTok for Developers for the FM2025 app.
